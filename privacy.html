WedPress – Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

WedPress does not collect, send or sell any personal information. Your guest list, seating plan, photos and settings are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device unless you choose to save a backup, export a file or print.

WedPress has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Purchases of WedPress Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store. WedPress only asks the Store whether Pro is owned; it never sees your payment details.

You can delete all your data at any time with ☰ More → Start a new empty plan, or by uninstalling the app.
