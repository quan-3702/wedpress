💍 How to use WedPress

WedPress helps you plan who sits where at your wedding, and prints everything with matching designs: the seating chart, place cards, escort cards, table numbers, save-the-dates, invitations and RSVP cards. Everything is saved on this computer only.

Want to look around first? Click ☰ More → Load the sample wedding. You can start a new empty plan from the same menu.

1. Add your guests

On the 👥 Guests tab, type your names and date at the top. Then type or paste guest names in the box, one guest on each line. Leave an empty line between families or groups of friends – WedPress keeps each group together at the same table.

You can also add the group and meal on the same line: John Carter, Bride's family, Chicken.

Already have a list in Excel or Google Sheets? Save it as CSV and click Import CSV / Excel list…. Columns called Name, Group, RSVP, Meal and Table are found automatically.

Click a guest's name to change the RSVP answer, meal, or to mark a child. The box at the bottom left counts who is attending and each meal for your caterer.

2. Seat everyone

On the 🪑 Seating tab, add tables (round or long) and choose the number of seats. Then either:

  • click ✨ Seat everyone automatically – groups stay together and tables are added if needed, or
  • drag a name from the left onto a table. You can also click a name, then click a table.

Drag a table by its title to arrange the room. Click ✎ on a table to rename it (for example "Head table"), change seats or delete it. A green border means the table is full; red means too many people.

Guests who answered "Can't come" are not seated.

3. Print

On the 🖨 Print tab, pick what to print and a style. Use ‹ › to look at every page, then click 🖨 Print.

  • Seating chart – "Find your seat" lists names A to Z with the table; "By table" shows each table with its guests. Choose a size from a letter page up to a 24 × 36 in poster.
  • Place cards – fold-over tent cards, 4 per page. Tick "Show the meal" so waiters know who ordered what.
  • Escort cards – small flat cards with name and table, 10 per page.
  • Table numbers – 5 × 7 in, two per page.
  • Save the date and Invitation – 5 × 7 in cards; add your photo and words.
  • RSVP cards – 4 per page.
  • Caterer list – guests by table with their meal and the meal totals.

In the print window, set Scale: 100% (or "Actual size") and turn off "Fit to page" so cards come out the right size. Card stock (around 65–110 lb / 176–300 gsm) looks best.

4. Big posters

For an 18 × 24 or 24 × 36 in seating chart you can:

  • click 💾 Save as picture and take the file to a print shop or upload it to an online photo printing service, or
  • click 🖨 Print to print it on several Letter or A4 sheets with overlap and cut marks, then tape them together on foam board.

5. Keep your plan safe

Your plan is saved automatically on this computer. Use ☰ More → Save a backup file now and then, to keep a copy or move it to another PC (open it with Open a backup file…). Export guest list makes a CSV file for Excel.
